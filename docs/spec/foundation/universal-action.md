# Foundation · Universal Action

The operating backbone. **Every** task, snag, approval, gap, delay, payment chase, document request, and AI recommendation normalizes into one `Action` object. Roles do not invent their own work-item types — they create typed Actions. `My Day` and every functional queue are just filtered views over Actions.

## 2. Prioritization — how My Day ranks

Actions are ranked by a transparent, rule-first score (AI may refine later but must not hide drivers):

```
rank = f(
  deadline_proximity(sla_due_at, plan_date),
  customer_impact(severity.customer),
  revenue_impact(business_impact.rupee_exposure),
  dependency_impact(count & criticality of downstream blocked),
  escalation_risk(sla_ladder_level)
)
```

Every ranked Action shows its **`why_now`** in plain language ("Overdue demand ₹12L, bank release expected Fri, blocks registration slot"). No unexplained scores.

**My Day one-click actions:** call · WhatsApp · request document · approve · assign · escalate · create recovery plan. Focus modes: by customer, by project, by functional queue. Daily closure summary + carry-forward reason.

## 3. SLA ladder — L0 to L4

| Level | Trigger | System response |
|---|---|---|
| **L0** | Within SLA | Owner executes; dashboard only. |
| **L1** | Approaching SLA / forecast risk | Owner + backup notified; recommended recovery action. |
| **L2** | SLA breached / customer milestone impacted | Department manager owns recovery plan. |
| **L3** | Repeated breach / material financial or customer impact | Functional head intervention. |
| **L4** | Critical customer / reputation / legal / safety impact | CX / COO / management alert **with decision pack**. |

**Decision pack** (auto-generated at L2+): what happened · current impact · critical dependencies · actions already taken · owner · next deadline · recommended decision · evidence links.

### The Escalation entity (producer side of H11)

An escalation is **auto-generated by the shared Action/SLA kernel** whenever the ladder reaches L2+ — no individual role hand-writes it, which is why H11 is "any role → management." The kernel is the producer; `management` is the consumer ([`handshakes.md`](handshakes.md) H11).

## 4. SLA ≠ Plan — the two clocks

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SLA** | Allowed service time for the Action (policy). |
| **Plan** | When the record should occur per the Project journey. |

Both computed independently. A task can be **within SLA but late to plan**, or vice versa — the UI shows both. Derived statuses: `On Track · Due Soon · At Risk · Overdue · Completed On Time · Completed Late · Paused/Waiting`.

**Date model** (shared with the journey engine — see [`architecture.md`](architecture.md) §Journey): every timed Action/stage carries `baseline_start/end` (immutable except controlled reset), `current_plan_start/end`, `forecast_start/end`, `actual_start/end`. Changing a planned date stores baseline, revision, actor, timestamp, reason, approval — **never** overwrites history.

## 5. Action sources

Actions are created by:

- **Manual** — a user creates a task.
- **Workflow** — a stage transition or handshake (see [`handshakes.md`](handshakes.md)) spawns Actions (e.g. CRM acceptance auto-generates onboarding Actions).
- **Rule** — a gate change, SLA breach, missing document, or overdue demand generates an Action.
- **AI** — an engine (journey risk, collection risk, commitment risk, sentiment) emits an `ai_recommendation` Action. AI Actions are **suggestions**; consequential steps still require human approval. AI may never auto-send consequential customer communication.

Every Action, whatever the source, is the **same object** with the same states, SLA, evidence, and audit — so one queue, one My Day, one analytics roll-up.

---

## 6. Audit

Every change, comment, reassignment, breach, and approval is timestamped with actor and emitted to the [`event-log.md`](event-log.md). Analytics roll up by Project, stage, department, role, owner, unit, and customer/booking.

---

## 7. Key behaviours (acceptance-testable)

1. Every actionable record appears in the Universal Action engine with owner, SLA, and evidence requirement. (#3)
2. An Action cannot close without its required evidence. (#3)
3. SLA health and Plan health can differ and are both shown, correctly explained. (§34 test)
4. Changing a planned date preserves baseline + revision history. (§34)
5. Every My Day item shows a plain-language "why now." (§11)
6. AI recommendations are Actions requiring human action, never auto-executed for consequential steps. (§13)
