WHEREAS the Developer is the absolute and legal owner of the premises described in the "Unit & Property Particulars" section below, forming part of the project titled {{ project.name }} at {{ project.location }}, having developed the same in accordance with the sanctioned building plans and applicable statutory approvals;

AND WHEREAS the Purchaser having inspected the said unit and being satisfied as to the developer's title and quality of construction, has agreed to purchase the said unit for a consideration set out under "Commercial Terms" and on the terms and conditions recorded herein, executed pursuant to and in furtherance of the Agreement of Sale dated {{ dates.agreement_date }} entered into between the parties;

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH that in consideration of the payments received or to be received by the Developer from the Purchaser under the terms of the said Agreement of Sale, the Developer hereby transfers, conveys and assures unto the Purchaser, all right, title and interest in the said unit together with its appurtenant rights, undivided share in the common areas and facilities of the project, and the right to peaceful possession and enjoyment thereof, subject to the covenants recorded hereunder.

Covenants of the Developer

The Developer covenants that the title of the said unit is clear, marketable and free from any encumbrance save as disclosed, that all statutory dues in respect of the said unit have been paid up to the date of registration, and that the Developer shall, upon such registration, hand over vacant, peaceful and legal possession of the said unit to the Purchaser subject to receipt of the full consideration and completion of the snagging process to the reasonable satisfaction of the Purchaser.

Covenants of the Purchaser

The Purchaser covenants that all payments towards the consideration shall be made in accordance with the Payment Plan; that the Purchaser shall bear all applicable taxes, stamp duty, registration charges and incidental expenses; that the Purchaser shall abide by the association bye-laws, maintenance obligations and rules governing the common areas of the project.
